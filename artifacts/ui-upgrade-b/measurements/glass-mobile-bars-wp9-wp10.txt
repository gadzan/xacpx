WP-9 glass tiers + WP-10 mobile bars
====================================

Glass (WP-9)
------------
Before: `.glass` and `.glass-strong` existed in style.css but nothing used them
(dead weight), the real surfaces repeated `bg-glass-strong backdrop-blur-glass
backdrop-saturate-glass` per element, the mobile top-bar controls carried their own
24px-blur recipe, and the dimming layers mixed `backdrop-blur-sm` (4px) with
`backdrop-blur-[3px]`.

After, in `style.css` (one definition site, three roles):

  .glass-1        chrome bars          --glass-bg        (white .72 / dark .72)
  .glass-2        floating surfaces    --glass-bg-strong (white .84 / dark .84)
  .glass-control  small controls       --glass-control-bg (.88) + --glass-sheen + --glass-control-shadow

- All three share `--glass-blur` (20px) + `--glass-saturate`; the difference is
  opacity and shadow depth, not a second visual system.
- `.glass-control` is the former DirectBotPane local recipe, moved to the global
  token as-is (specular sheen, foreground hairline at 6-8% alpha, one wide low
  shadow). The pane's scoped rules keep only hover / press / focus-visible and the
  avatar disc's more opaque fill.
- Degrade: `@supports not (backdrop-filter)` and `prefers-reduced-transparency:
  reduce` both rewrite the tokens (all three to `--c-raised`, sheen none) and the
  latter also drops the filter. Same three classes either way.
- Scrim blur: new Tailwind token `backdropBlur.scrim` = 3px, used by the dialog
  shell, MobileSheet backdrop, ConfirmDialog, the subagent trace dialog, the
  command palette, the scheduled-tasks drawer and TopicSwitcher's two overlays.
  No `backdrop-blur-[3px]` override is left at a call site.

Mobile bars (WP-10)
-------------------
Three bars, one language: `glass-control` round discs + a name capsule.

  session bar  ← disc | [driver icon + name/subtitle] capsule | ⋯ disc
  Direct bar   ← disc | [56px avatar disc over the capsule] name | ⋯ disc
  Group bar    ← disc | [collage disc] name | [topic] capsule | ⋯ disc

- The instances' 52px height and safe-area padding are unchanged; all three keep
  the in-flow bar (nothing scrolls under it).
- Group needed one correction on the way: a 44px topic pill plus a title line inside
  one capsule overflowed the 52px bar and clipped the group name at the top of the
  screen. The topic pill is its own capsule beside the name capsule now — recorded
  because the first attempt was visibly broken.
- Every disc is 44px (WP-4 gate re-run: 12 mobile e2e tests green, including the
  four-midpoint hit tests on ←, ⋯ and the bar's capsule text).
