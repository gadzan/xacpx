WP-14 UiButton
==============

One component: src/components/UiButton.vue

Variants
--------
primary        bg-accent text-on-accent hover:bg-accent-hover      (solid fill)
secondary      border-hairline bg-raised text-fg hover:bg-fill-hover
ghost          text-fg-muted hover:bg-fill-hover hover:text-fg
danger         bg-danger text-on-danger hover:bg-danger/90          (solid fill)
danger-ghost   text-danger-text hover:bg-danger/10                 (inline destructive entry)

Sizes: sm = 28px (h-7 px-2.5 text-xs), md = 32px (h-8 px-3 text-ui). Radius rounded-btn (8px).
mobile-block = the dialog-footer shape: full width and 44 tall below lg, compact from lg.
Hit area: [@media(pointer:coarse)]:min-h-11 lifts the touch box to 44 without changing the
visual height; desktop density is untouched. Measured in the WP-4 pass.
`el` is exposed for callers that own their focus (ComposerConfirmDialog's safe default).

Contrast
--------
Primary reuses the WP-1/2 measurements: --c-on-accent on --c-accent (both themes) and
--c-on-danger on --c-danger for the danger variant; the ghost/danger-ghost text colors are the
already-measured fg-muted / danger-text pairs. No new color tokens were introduced.

Settings page size language
---------------------------
Before: add-instance was h-11 rounded-2xl, notifications was rounded-sm py-1, sign-out was a
rounded-lg text button. After: all three are UiButton (md) with the same 8px radius, 32px height
and text-ui scale; the danger entry uses danger-ghost.
